How to commission a celebration gâteau

A celebration gâteau starts with three questions: how many people it needs to serve, what the room will be like, and which flavours you love. The number of guests sets the number of tiers. The room matters more than people expect, because a mousse cake that is perfect at twenty degrees struggles in a warm marquee, and we will steer you towards a sturdier build if the day will be hot.

Then we taste. You choose two or three flavours from the collection, or describe one you have in mind, and we make small versions for you to try. This is the moment to tell us about allergies in your party. Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs and nuts, so we cannot promise a cake free from them, but we can leave any of them out of the recipe.

Give us at least two weeks for a tiered cake and seventy-two hours for a single gâteau. We assemble everything the day before, never earlier, and we deliver and set up tiered cakes ourselves. If you would rather collect, bring a flat, level space in the car and drive as if the cake were a sleeping baby.
